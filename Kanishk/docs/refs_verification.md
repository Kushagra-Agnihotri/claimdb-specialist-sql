Heads-up for compiling: BibTeX styles, including `acl_natbib.bst`, lower-case titles that are not protected by braces. ACL Anthology entries already protect acronyms with braces, but titles from DBLP, the proceedings sites and arXiv will print as, for example, "Tabfact", "Omnisql", "llm", "sql". To keep the capitals, wrap those words in braces (e.g. `{TabFact}`, `{LLM}`). That changes only typography, not content.
